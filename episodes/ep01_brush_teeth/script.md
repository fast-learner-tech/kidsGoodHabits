# Episode 1: Pip and the Sparkly Smile (v2, for approval)

- **Habit:** brushing teeth, morning and night
- **Ages:** 3-8
- **Target length:** about 160 seconds of picture (shot lengths are final once voices and the song are recorded and timed)
- **Cast:** Pip (6), Tomo (3), Nana Rue (grown-up), Biscuit (cat, sounds only), Narrator
- **Sets:** Pip's bedroom (morning), bathroom, Pip's bedroom (night)
- **Color script:** mint / aqua (fresh, sparkly); warm peach accents in the morning, soft indigo at night
- **Story spine:** Pip wakes with "fuzzy teeth" (the problem) → Tomo and Pip get it wrong in funny ways → Nana Rue shows the three steps → everyone tries → "Not fuzzy anymore!" (the payoff) → song recap → Pip brushes again at night

## The three steps (each said at least 3 times, plus once in the song)
1. **A tiny dot** of toothpaste, as small as a pea.
2. **Little circles** on the fronts, the backs and the chewing tops.
3. **Two whole minutes**, then spit it out.

**Catchphrase:** "It's Sparkly Smile time!" (said by Pip; becomes the show's recurring cue)

**The "why" (for the older viewers):** brushing sweeps away germs and bits of food that can hurt our teeth. Kept gentle on purpose: no scary "germ monsters".

**Health notes behind the script:** a pea-sized amount of toothpaste is the usual advice for ages 3-6; young children brush with a grown-up's help (Nana Rue helps both children, hands-on with Tomo); two minutes, twice a day; spit, don't swallow. Please check these against your local dental guidance before publishing.

## What changed from the draft
- **A clear setup and payoff:** "fuzzy teeth, like a sweater" (shot 3) pays off with "Not fuzzy anymore!" (shot 24), so the habit visibly solves Pip's problem.
- **Pip owns the catchphrase** ("It's Sparkly Smile time!") instead of the Narrator, which builds the character and gives the show a recurring hook.
- **Funnier mistakes:** the toothpaste mountain flops over; Pip's speed-brush becomes a "race car" Nana Rue can name kindly. Mistakes are corrected without anyone being told off.
- **Biscuit gets a mini-arc:** wakes Pip, gets a bubble on the nose, sneezes, spins.
- **A softer "why":** "sweeps away" instead of fighting germs, for the youngest viewers.
- **The chorus is now a real song** with a cleaner rhyme scheme (pea/me, ptoo/you) and is split between characters so each one sings.
- **One voice at a time in the song:** each line is a solo (Pip, Tomo, Nana Rue, Pip). Two text-to-speech voices on the same line sounded doubled and overlapping.
- **Night bookend makes the "twice a day" point:** Pip has brushed again, not just announced it.

## Notes and open points for you
